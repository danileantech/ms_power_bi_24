3. Using examples
4. Customizing examples

Consider age related design:
- Children
- Adults
- Major adults

### 2. Highlight insights
- Focus in relevant data points
- Highlight outliers
- Provide correct level of detail

## Tools
**Theme**: provide consistency, maintain the brand identity, and get a better user experience

**Custom Theme**: to create a fully customized theme.

**Conditional Formatting**: to dynamically change the color of your visual based on specific conditions or rules.

**Use of diverging colors**: highlight the positive and negative values within the scale.

**Font choices**: ensure easy readability across devices.

**Whitespace utilization**: declutter appearance of reports.

**Borders and lines**:  helps separate various sections, highlight key messages, and guide the viewer’s eye through the report.

**Alignment and grids**: to create a neat and structured layout.

**Background/Images**: complement your report’s theme.

**Responsive design**: easily adapts to various screen sizes.
