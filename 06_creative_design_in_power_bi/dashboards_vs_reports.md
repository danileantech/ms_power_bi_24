# Dashboards vs Reports
### Dashboard
It's a one page group of visuals showing high level overview of kpis and insights. Its composed of:
- Executive summary
- Sales performance
- Market overview
- customer metrics
- operational performance
- strategic initiatives

### Report
It's a in-depth analysis of the data, allowing drill-down and drill-through functionalitites. A report can be distributed in multiple pages, each one with its type of visualization:
- sales performance overview
- geographical analysis
- product analysis
- sales person performance
- time-based analysis
